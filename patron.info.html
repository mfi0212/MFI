About the Name Change

The service originally operated under the name MFI. On October 20, 2023, Black Swan acquired 50% ownership of the service.

After Black Swan became a 50% owner, the service name was changed from MFI to BS&MFI to reflect its connection with Black Swan.

BS&MFI continues to provide lending services, while Black Swan provides data storage services.
