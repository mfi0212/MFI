About Us

  • BotPay takes the required amount, pays the fee for the selected services, and adds the amounts directly to your account.
  • It simplifies fee payment and credit so you can complete transactions in fewer steps.

How BotPay works?

  • Choose the service or amount you want to process.
  • BotPay calculates and pays the applicable fee.
  • The remaining or credited amount is added directly to your account.
  • Fast and transparent — you see the fee and final credit before confirming.
Note: The fee is deducted before the amount is credited. Ensure sufficient balance or payment method is available to complete the transaction.

FAQ's

What does BotPay do exactly?

It takes the amount, pays the service fee, and adds the processed amount directly to your account.

When is the fee charged?

The fee is paid as part of the same transaction, before the final amount is credited to your account.

How long does it take for the amount to appear?

In most cases the amount is added shortly after successful fee payment and confirmation.

Can I use BotPay for multiple services at once?

Yes, where the system allows. Each service fee will be calculated and shown clearly before you confirm.

What if the fee payment fails?

If the fee cannot be paid, the amount will not be credited. You can retry after fixing the payment method or balance.

Is there any extra charge for using BotPay?

Only the standard service fee is charged. There is no additional BotPay processing fee unless clearly stated for a specific offer.
