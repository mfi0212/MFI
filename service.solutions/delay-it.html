FAQ's

What is the minimum amount I can extend?

The minimum amount is ₹500.

How is the fee calculated?

The fee of the amount multiplied by the number of days you choose.

Can I apply for multiple extensions?

Yes, you can apply multiple times. Each extension is treated separately with its own fee.

If today is my return date, can I still extend?

No, you must apply at least 24 hours before the original return date.

How many days can I extend?

Standard range is 15–30 days. Premium members can go up to 60 days.

Will my credit score be affected?

No. Delay It is a paid service and does not impact your credit report.
