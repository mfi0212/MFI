About Us

  • BsRora lets you apply for amounts and offers, and pay the required fees automatically without any manual involvement from the member side.
  • The system handles the full application and fee payment process in the background for a smooth experience.

How BsRora works?

  • Select the amount or offer you want to apply for.
  • Confirm and the system automatically processes the fee payment.
  • No need for the member to sit and complete each step manually.
  • Transparent process with clear status updates.
Note: The applicable fee must be available in your account balance before applying. If the fee is not paid, the application cannot be processed.

FAQ's

What can I apply for with BsRora?

You can apply for available amounts and special offers listed in the system.

Do I need to pay the fee myself every time?

No. Once you confirm, BsRora automatically deducts and pays the fee without further member involvement.

What if my balance is insufficient for the fee?

The application will not go through. Please ensure enough balance is available before applying.

How long does the process take?

Most applications are processed within a few minutes after confirmation.

Can I cancel an application after submitting?

Cancellation depends on the stage of processing. Contact support if the request is still pending.

Is there any extra charge for automatic processing?

No. Only the standard fee for the amount or offer is charged. There are no hidden auto-processing charges.
