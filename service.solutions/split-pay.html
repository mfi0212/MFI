  • With Split half pay, you can pay half of the total amount and extend the remaining half with a flexibility while ensuring smooth repayment.

How Half Split Pay works?

  • Paying 50% of the amount now and split the remaining 50% in instalments.
  • Fast approval and simple process.
Note: The split fee must be paid upfront along with the first 50%. This allows you to split the remaining amount.

FAQ's

What is the minimum amount I can split?

The minimum amount is ₹2000.

How is the fee divided?

The fee is shared equally among all participants.

Can I choose different number of splits?

Yes — you can select between 2 to 8 splits.
