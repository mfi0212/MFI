FAQ's

Can I pre-save whenever I want?

Yes, if you are VIP then you can use it whenever you want.

Where can I find the fee for Pre-Saver?

The fee for Pre-Saver is based on the offer or amount you are applying (ask coordinator).

Can I pre-save whenever I want? But I'm not VIP!

No, but you can apply whenever an offer is available soon.

Is there any withdrawal fee?

No fee if you withdraw after 30 days. Early withdrawal has a 1% fee.

Can I set different rules for different accounts?

Yes, you can create multiple Pre-Saver goals (Travel, Emergency, etc.).

How secure is my saved money?

100% secure with bank-level encryption and insurance up to ₹5 lakh.
