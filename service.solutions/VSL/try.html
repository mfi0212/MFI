Frequently Asked Questions

Make sure you enter the correct account password. You can log in only when you have an active borrowing or lending record registered in VSL. If you believe your record is active, contact your coordinator.
VSL is a service from BS&MFI that helps verified account holders view their borrowing and lending records, track interest and maintain personal notes.
To become a lender, your account must first be officially verified by a representative from VSL. Contact your coordinator or a VSL representative for verification and further steps. You cannot become an official lender simply by creating an account.
To become a borrower, your account must be officially verified by a representative from VSL. Contact your coordinator or a VSL representative to discuss the process and verification. Borrowing access is subject to approval.
Verification helps VSL confirm that the lender or borrower is officially registered. Only accounts verified by an authorised VSL representative can be recognised as official lender or borrower accounts.
VSL Premium+ provides additional display and customisation features. It unlocks Borrowed By, Interest Rate, Date Borrowed and Days Passed details, allows you to control detail popups, switch between Light and Dark (AMOLED) themes and choose your own main colour.
Contact your coordinator and request Premium+ activation. Once your Premium+ status is activated on your VSL account, the additional features will become available.
Some account details are exclusive to Premium+ members. Upgrade to Premium+ to unlock Borrowed By, Interest Rate, Date Borrowed and Days Passed information.
Contact your coordinator or submit the VSL Issue Form so the issue can be reviewed.
Contact your VSL coordinator or use the Issue Form if you need help with login, account records or other VSL-related problems.
