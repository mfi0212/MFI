FAQs

How much was limit to borrow from BS&MFI Services? +

Borrow limit will be based on the subscription plans.

How long should I borrow a amount for? +

In our services borrower should take money for atleast 15 days.
But by STL (Short term Loans) you can borrow between 1-5 days.

How to apply for a amount? +

On every services there is an option ‘Apply’, click on the option and fill out the form and submit.

What if I'm late in returning the amount? +

A penalty interest will be added to your borrow. Learn more..

Where can I see my interest details? +

You can check your interest details in the ‘My Account’ section.

How long does it take to receive the money after submitting the form? +

It may take a few hours to gather the money.

How can i get the money from services? +

Before applying for a amount, you must be close to one of us our authorized distributors. If you are a new, we cannot offer a high amount initially.

How can i conform my amount is arranging? +

Once your account is activated on the site, you can be assured that your amount is being arranged. If the amount is not added within an hour, it means we are not unable to arrange the amount.
