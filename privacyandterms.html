Privacy Policy

At BS&MFI Services, we understand that customers share personal and financial information when borrowing money. We use this information to provide our lending services, manage loans and meet our legal responsibilities.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for or use our lending services, we may collect the following information, depending on the service:

  • Your name, date of birth, phone number and email address.
  • Your home address and identity verification details.
  • Your income, employment and other financial details needed for a loan.
  • Bank account or payment details needed to process loan payments.
  • Loan applications, loan amounts, repayments and outstanding balances.
  • Documents or other information you provide to us.
  • Information needed to protect our services and prevent fraud.

We collect information that is relevant to the service you use and handle it according to applicable rule.

2. Why We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Review and process your loan application.
  • Check your identity and verify the details you provide.
  • Decide whether you qualify for a loan.
  • Provide the loan and manage your account.
  • Calculate amounts due and maintain repayment records.
  • Contact you about your loan, payments or account.
  • Prevent fraud and protect our customers and services.
  • Meet legal, accounting and other required obligations.
  • Handle questions, requests and complaints.

3. How We Store Your Information

Customer information may be stored in our business systems or with authorised service providers who help us operate our lending business.

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell customer personal information for advertising. We may share information when necessary for our lending business or when required or permitted by rule.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Payment providers and banks involved in loan transactions.
  • Identity verification and fraud prevention providers.
  • Authorised service providers helping us manage our business.

We only share information for relevant purposes and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep customer information for as long as needed to provide our services, manage loans, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. We may need to retain certain information even after a loan has been repaid.

When information is no longer needed, it will be deleted or handled as required by applicable rule.

6. Keeping Your Information Safe

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials, verification codes and other confidential information safe. Do not share them with unauthorised people.

If you believe someone has accessed your account or information without permission, contact us as soon as possible.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable rule, you may ask us to provide access to your personal information, correct inaccurate details, delete information that no longer needs to be kept, or help you exercise other available privacy rights.

We may ask you to verify your identity before handling a request. We may also need to keep certain information where required by rule.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our services may contain links to other websites or use third-party payment or technology services. Those services may have their own privacy policies. Read their policies before providing information directly to them.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
